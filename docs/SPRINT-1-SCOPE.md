# Sprint 1 scope

Version: `ADMIN-0.2.0 / TIME-0.2.0`. Status: controlled acceptance candidate, not accepted or frozen.

## Included

- Dynamic browser-local Employee Register with effective-dated assignments, aliases, active state, controlled within-band ordering and secondary backup/restore.
- IndexedDB persistence for the approved April-to-March annual workbook, with replace/remove controls.
- New-employee detection and protected match/create workflow.
- Local ZIP/XLSX ingestion for a selected reporting month.
- TIME-DATA-001 deterministic hours authority with retained audit values.
- Project, internal and exception classification; protected catalogue suggestions and explicit match/genuine-uncoded decisions.
- Project consolidation, internal separation, description-conflict detection and full reconciliation.
- Styled project workbook based on the supplied master/template.
- Separate confidential Internal Hours workbook with totals and audit trace.
- Exact creator attribution and approved portrait on the About page.
- Synthetic-only deployed base data plus an interim, separately tokened, client-side encrypted Employee Viewer publication link/file and encrypted local month library.

## Excluded

Invoices, rates, cost calculations, carryover propagation, historical invoice lifecycle, company/individual authentication, central publication storage, cloud database, telemetry, payroll, broad HR functionality, TPT/DFT changes and Sprint 2.

Carry and notes columns are intentionally left blank/manual. Real unresolved exceptions and project-description conflicts require Office Manager action before export.
